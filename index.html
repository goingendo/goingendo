Frequently asked questions

What is Going Endo?

Going Endo is an emerging technology education and innovation company. It helps businesses and industry partners apply new technologies to real challenges.

Who does Going Endo work with?

Companies, industry associations, economic development organizations, universities, and research institutions.

How is Going Endo different?

Instead of teaching technology in isolation, Going Endo brings companies, experts, and regional partners together around a real industry challenge, then helps develop, test, and measure practical solutions.

Which technologies do you cover?

Artificial intelligence, automation, data, energy technologies, and other emerging technologies relevant to the industry.

Where does Going Endo work?

Going Endo is based in Salt Lake City, Utah, and is built to work with industries internationally.

Has Going Endo launched?

Going Endo is preparing to launch and is inviting its first industry partners now.

How can my organization work with Going Endo?

Share a challenge using the form on this page or email hello@goingendo.com.

What does the name mean?

Going Endo means going within: working inside companies and industries rather than apart from them.

Is Going Endo affiliated with a university or institute?

No. Going Endo LLC is an independent company.
