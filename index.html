FAQ

What is Going Endo?

Going Endo helps leadership teams choose the AI use case worth pursuing, one that pays off for the business and for the customers, workers, and suppliers it depends on.

What is the AI Briefing?

A single online session for the member companies of an association, chamber, or innovation agency. It shows what AI can do in their industry right now.

What is the AI Workshop?

An online or in person workshop for one company's leadership team. You leave with a written assessment of one AI use case: the value to your business and the people it affects, the risks, and a plan to test it.

Do you build or sell technology?

No. We help you choose. When the work needs specialists, we bring them in.

Who is it for?

Leadership teams of established companies, and the associations, chambers, and agencies that support them.

Where is it available?

The United States, the United Arab Emirates, Singapore, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland. Online or in person.

Which technologies?

AI first. Then connected devices, robotics, and immersive technology where they fit.

What does it cost?

Briefing arrangements are agreed with each partner. Workshop details come in a first conversation.

Have you launched?

Not yet. We're inviting our first partners and clients now.

What does the name mean?

Going within. We work inside your company and your industry.

Are you affiliated with a university or institute?

No. Going Endo LLC is independent.
