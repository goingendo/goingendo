AI Opportunity Brief

For a fictional manufacturer

The problem.

Unplanned machine stops on the main production line.

What it costs today.

Lost output, overtime to catch up, and rush orders for spare parts.

The AI use case.

Use the sensor data the machines already collect to predict breakdowns before they happen.

Payoff for the business.

Less downtime, fewer emergency repairs, and steadier output.

Payoff for your people and partners.

Safer, calmer shifts for operators. More reliable deliveries for customers and suppliers.

Risks.

Sensor data may be patchy. Operators need to trust the alerts before they act on them.

The smallest test.

Run predictions on one line and compare its stops with a similar line that runs as usual.
