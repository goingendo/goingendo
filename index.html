Frequently asked questions

What is Going Endo?

Going Endo is an emerging technology education company. We bring experts into your company, teach your leadership team what is changing, and help you test one real idea on a real problem.

Who is it for?

Leadership teams at companies with roughly 100 to 5,000 employees, and the chambers, industry groups, and universities that support them.

How does it start?

With a free briefing for your leadership team. If there is a good fit, we plan a short program and an applied project together.

Which technologies do you cover?

Artificial intelligence first, along with automation, robotics, and other emerging technologies that matter to your industry.

Where do you work?

Going Endo is based in Utah and works with companies across the United States, on site or online.

How is this different from a conference or an online course?

You learn on your own problem, inside your own company, and you leave with a pilot you can measure.

What does it cost?

The briefing is free. Program pricing depends on the scope and is shared after the briefing.

Has Going Endo launched?

Going Endo is preparing to launch and is inviting its first company partners now.

What does the name mean?

Going Endo means going within. We work inside your company instead of sending your leaders somewhere else to learn.

Is Going Endo affiliated with any university or institute?

No. Going Endo LLC is an independent company.
