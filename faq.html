Questions and answers.

What is Going Endo?

Going Endo helps leadership teams choose the AI use case worth pursuing, one that pays off for the business and the people it depends on, then build it.

What is the AI Briefing?

A single online session for the member companies of an association, chamber, or innovation agency. It shows what AI can do in their industry right now. There is no cost to the host organization.

What is the AI Workshop?

An online or in person workshop for one company's leadership team. You leave with an AI Opportunity Brief for one AI use case: the value to your business and the people it affects, the risks, and a plan to test it.

Do you build the AI?

Yes, through an AI Sprint. Our specialist partners build and test the use case you chose. We manage the project and stay accountable to you.

What is an AI Sprint?

A focused project that builds and tests the use case from your AI Opportunity Brief. Scope and price are agreed after the workshop.

Who is it for?

Leadership teams of established companies, and the associations, chambers, and agencies that support them.

Where is it available?

The United States, the United Arab Emirates, Singapore, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland. Online or in person.

Which technologies?

AI first. Then connected devices, robotics, and immersive technology where they fit.

What does it cost?

Briefings are free for host organizations. Workshop details come in a first conversation.

Have you launched?

Not yet. We're inviting our first partners and clients now.

What does the name mean?

Going within. We work inside your company and your industry.

Who is behind Going Endo?

Matthew Hill, founder. He started Going Endo to help leaders answer one question: what should we actually do with AI?
