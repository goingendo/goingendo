Privacy Policy

This page explains what we collect when you join the Going Endo launch community and what we do with it.

Effective October 24, 2026

What we collect

When you use the signup form on goingendo.com we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The interests you check, such as attending a session or partnering with us
  • The technology you most want to understand, if you choose to tell us

We do not ask for anything else, and you never need an account to use this site.

Who processes it

Our signup form is run by Formspree. When you submit the form your information goes to Formspree, which stores it and forwards it to us by email. Formspree handles it under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

We use your information only to send you Going Endo updates and event news, such as session dates, speakers, ticket releases, and founding pricing. Your topic suggestions help us choose future sessions.

We never sell it

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not share it with anyone except the service that runs our form.

Other services this site uses

The site is hosted by Vercel and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like most websites these services may receive basic technical details such as your IP address and browser type when the page loads.

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. It sets cookies and collects usage data such as the pages you visit, your approximate location, and your device type. We use it only to improve the site, and we never send your name or email address to Google.

You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add on.

Unsubscribe or delete your information

Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. You can also email hello@goingendo.com at any time to unsubscribe, ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. We will take care of your request promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@goingendo.com.
